Method and caveats

  • Probabilities. Match result: the de-vigged bookmaker odds (power method). Double chance: sums of those. Over/under 2.5: the bookmaker's over/under price, recalibrated. Over/under 1.5, each team to score and both teams to score: Poisson scoring rates implied by the match odds, recalibrated. Every recalibration was fitted on past seasons and tested on later ones (the "historical backtest" column above).
  • Astrology agrees counts the classical engines (Rao, Chokoisky, Frawley) whose predicted score agrees with the pick. Across tens of thousands of past matches, agreement has not improved hit rates beyond what the odds implied.
  • Scoring. Results are after 90 minutes. Picks made after kickoff and void games are listed but not scored. The chance that several picks all come in multiplies their probabilities, which assumes the games are independent.
  • Records. Live-record forecasts are sealed before kickoff with a SHA-256 hash; games added manually are not sealed. Your picks are stored on your own computer.
