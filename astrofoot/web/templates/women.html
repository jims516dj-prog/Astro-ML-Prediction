Women's football

A separate section for women's competitions: its own forecasts, sealed before kickoff and scored like the men's, in its own ledger (ledger/women), never mixed into the men's track record, Top picks, My picks or Dataset. Fixtures and odds come from ESPN (women's competitions with odds are discovered daily), results from ESPN. Also under Live record → Women (full filters) and Top picks → Women; build slips under My picks → Women.

Read with care. Engine 4 and the goal-market recalibrations were trained on men's football, so these probabilities rest mainly on the bookmaker odds; the classical engines are shown as for the men. Grounds are the venues ESPN names for each game (women's teams often play away from the men's stadiums), and form checks use women's results only. The track record below shows how well this holds up.
