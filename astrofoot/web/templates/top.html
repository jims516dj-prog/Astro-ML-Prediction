{% else %}

Up to 10 matches a day where one team is most likely to score at least once: the home team or the away team, whichever is likelier. The market does not price this directly here, so it is derived from the match odds (the scoring rates that reproduce the home-win and away-win probabilities) and recalibrated on {{ "{:,}".format(ts.n_matches) if ts else "historical" }} past matches. "Astrology agrees" counts how many of the three classical engines' predicted scores have that team scoring.

{% endif %}
