{% elif goals %}

Every forecast match of the day with an over/under price, ranked by how probable over or under 2.5 goals is, from the sealed pre-kickoff forecasts. The probability is the market's over/under price, recalibrated on {{ "{:,}".format(ou.n_matches) if ou.n_matches else "historical" }} past matches (bookmaker prices understate the likelier side by a little), so "expected" below is an honest target. Each classical engine predicts a score; "astrology agrees" counts how many of the three predict the same side of 2.5.

{% elif scorer %}

Every forecast match of the day, ranked by how likely one team is to score at least once: the home team or the away team, whichever is likelier. The market does not price this directly here, so it is derived from the match odds (the scoring rates that reproduce the home-win and away-win probabilities) and recalibrated on {{ "{:,}".format(ts.n_matches) if ts else "historical" }} past matches. "Astrology agrees" counts how many of the three classical engines' predicted scores have that team scoring.

{% elif btts %}

Every forecast match of the day, ranked by how probable both teams to score, yes or no, is. Like home or away goal, it is derived from the match odds (the Poisson scoring rates behind the home-win and away-win probabilities) and recalibrated on the three most recent seasons, because the both-score rate drifts ({{ fmt_pct(ts.btts.rate) if ts and ts.btts else "" }} overall, {{ fmt_pct(ts.btts.recent_rate) if ts and ts.btts else "" }} lately). It is the hardest of the four: even the best picks are only a little better than a coin flip. "Astrology agrees" counts the engines whose predicted score has both (or not both) teams scoring.

{% elif dc %}

Every forecast match of the day, ranked by its likeliest double chance: home or draw (1X), away or draw (X2), or home or away, i.e. no draw (12). The probability is the sum of two de-vigged match-odds probabilities, which historically needs no correction ({{ fmt_pct(research.all.hit_rate) if research else "" }} of {{ "{:,}".format(research.all.n) if research else "" }} picks won vs {{ fmt_pct(research.all.expected) if research else "" }} claimed). "Astrology agrees" counts the engines whose predicted result falls inside the pick.

{% else %}
